## Execution fixed grid, not optimized
- Entry: next completed signal minute OPEN, assumed market order filled **2 adverse ticks** (0.50 NQ points) worse than raw opening quote. Contract units: separately 1 NQ (USD 20/pt, round-trip NinjaTrader Free all-in commission $5.74) and 1 MNQ (USD 2/pt, round-trip NinjaTrader Free commission $1.88). Historical **NQ price paths** used to simulate both; MNQ-specific liquidity/quotes not independently verified.
- Stop width grid: **0.10, 0.15, 0.20 × past 14-session median RTH range** (minimum 4 ticks = 1.0 index point). Use fixed price levels computed from the entry fill. Target grid: **1.0, 1.5, 2.0 R** (target distance as multiple of stop width). Time exit grid: **30, 60 minutes from market entry**. Total **3×3×2=18 configurations** per signal, 72 per instrument. Do not add/filter/change scenarios after results.
- At each next 1m bar, process **open gaps first**; a stop gap executes no better than that bar's open; targets have at-best limit fill at the target, not favorable opening price improvement. When intrabar low/high simultaneously touches both stop and TP, **stop executes first (pessimistic)**. A stop/target touch on the entry bar is eligible; where entry bar opening and its OHLC movement sequence are unknowable, flag 1-minute ambiguity and use pessimistic stop precedence.
- Stop is a stop-market approximation (not tick-accurate). Exit price receives **2 adverse ticks** in all cases (stop, target or time), deliberately conservative even for limit target; one full round-trip commission is subtracted. For stops beyond the barrier opening: adverse gap exit open plus adverse slippage. For targets, no optimistic gap-through target reward.
- Exit on time at minute OPEN exactly 30 or 60 bars after entry-open if SL/TP not previously touched; do not score exit-minute high/low once time cutoff occurs. Market paths remain within the same complete RTH session.
- Stress: 1.5 × modeled transaction friction (all-in commission + four adverse ticks) as sensitivity, and NQ one-trade nominal stop-loss amount relative to $2,000 MFFU eval drawdown. Do not claim Free broker fees are actual MFFU costs or that nominal stops account for gap/slippage/floating drawdown.
