## Complete family: precisely 20 fixed directional tests

**Block A — Initial-range anatomy and interaction**
- **NQ-H33 (09:45):** first-15 range ≥1.20× historical `R15m`; first-15 close in top 15% or bottom 15% of its high-low (mutually exclusive); trade in direction of extreme close, **continuation**.
- **NQ-H34 (10:00):** initial 15-min range ≤0.70× past20 `R15m`; 10:00 close at least one tick above that initial high or below that initial low; enter **breakout continuation**; no both-sides ambiguity at a single close.
- **NQ-H35 (10:00):** absolute first15 close–first15 OPEN ≥0.20 `A`, then 10:00 close retraces between 30% and 65% (inclusive) of initial 15-min net body away from initial close towards initial open; enter **continuation of original body**.
- **NQ-H36 (10:00):** first15 absolute opening body ≥0.15 `A`, and the 09:46–10:00 net move is **opposite** first15 body and ≥0.15 `A`; follow **reversal** at 10:00.
- **NQ-H37 (09:45):** first15 range ≥0.15 `A`; an upper wick ≥50% of first15 range and close in bottom 30% -> SHORT, or lower wick ≥50% and close in top 30% -> LONG; ambiguous both conditions skipped. Trade **against dominant wick**.

**Block B — Morning price-path geometry**
- **NQ-H38 (10:15):** efficiency `abs(C10:15–O09:31) / sum(abs(consecutive 1m CLOSE changes 09:31–10:15))` ≥0.60, and absolute C10:15–O09:31 ≥0.25 `A`; follow signed morning body.
- **NQ-H39 (10:15):** same efficiency ≤0.15 and morning body abs ≥0.10 `A`; **fade** signed morning body.
- **NQ-H40 (10:15):** last-five (10:11–10:15) signed close-to-close returns have four or more moves in the same direction (0s excluded); first-15 and last-five directions opposite; trade in direction of last-five **reversal**. Ret interval uses strictly bars 10:10 to 10:15.
- **NQ-H41 (10:15):** C10:15 exceeds 09:31–10:00 high by ≥1 tick -> LONG, or falls below 09:31–10:00 low by ≥1 tick -> SHORT; **accepted morning half-hour breakout continuation**.
- **NQ-H42 (10:30):** C10:15 is outside initial 09:31–10:00 high/low by ≥1 tick, then C10:30 strictly back inside that same interval; side is **opposite** the original 10:15 breakout. Once both sides are ambiguous, skip.

**Block C — Volume distribution (not isolated 6-minute volume shock tested earlier)**
- **NQ-H43 (09:45):** volume first15 ≥1.40× prior20 corresponding interval median, first15 body abs ≥0.15 `A`; follow first15 body.
- **NQ-H44 (09:45):** volume first15 ≤0.65× prior20 corresponding interval median, first15 body abs ≥0.15 `A`; **fade** first15 body.
- **NQ-H45 (10:00):** 09:46–10:00 volume ≥1.30× historical same-window volume median; 10:00 close is *inside* initial 09:31–09:45 high-low, second15 body abs ≥0.10 `A`; follow second15 body.
- **NQ-H46 (10:00):** 09:46–10:00 volume ≥1.40× historical second15 volume median; its high exceeded first15 high by ≥1 tick but C10:00 closed strictly below first15 high (SHORT), or low fell under first15 low by ≥1 tick but C10:00 closed strictly above first15 low (LONG). Skip sessions that satisfy both sides. **Range-boundary rejection**.
- **NQ-H47 (10:00):** second15 volume ratio vs its own historical median ≤0.70× the first15 volume ratio vs its historical median; second15 body abs ≥0.12 `A`; fade second15 body (waning participation).

**Block D — Previous-session regime geometry (requires consistent contract for levels)**
- **NQ-H48 (09:45):** previous RTH close in top 20% (prior bullish) or bottom 20% (prior bearish) of its own high-low; opening gap vs prior close opposite that previous bias ≥0.15 `A`; first15 body aligned with the gap and abs ≥0.10 `A`; follow **contrarian gap**. Same-contract required.
- **NQ-H49 (09:45):** prior RTH high strictly below second-prior RTH high AND prior low strictly above second-prior RTH low (inside day); first15 body abs ≥0.12 `A`; trade first15 body continuation. Same contract 3 session days.
- **NQ-H50 (09:45):** prior high strictly above second-prior high AND prior low strictly below second-prior low (outside day); prior close 35–65% of prior high-low; first15 body abs ≥0.12 `A`; trade **fade** first15 body. Same contract 3 session days.
- **NQ-H51 (09:45):** previous 3 completed RTH daily ranges strictly decreasing from oldest to newest; current first15 range ≥ past20 median R15m; first15 body abs ≥0.10 `A`; enter continuation of body. Same contract previous 3 days.
- **NQ-H52 (09:45):** previous 3 daily close-to-close steps strictly same sign; current first15 body opposite that signed trend and abs ≥0.10 `A`; follow current reversal body. Same contract previous 4 close observations.
