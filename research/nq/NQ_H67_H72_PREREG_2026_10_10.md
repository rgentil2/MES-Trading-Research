## Deduction from documented earlier evidence
- H09 gap-direction continuation: N=173, +$117.38 per NQ event at +30m, q=.706, concentrated in 2022. Profitable-looking H09 stop brackets were inspected on reused data and are not confirmations.
- H55 overnight VWAP convergence: N=178, +$47.43/NQ +30m, q=.632, but the prespecified bracket had negative expectation.
- H56 premarket reversal: N=100, +$75.56/NQ +30m, q=.632; a predeclared bracket was positive but closed DD for one NQ >$2k, so 1 MNQ is used in this experiment and prop-account safety is NOT assumed.
- H55 AND H56 fired on 67 shared sessions, same side. H59 had +$111.50/NQ on those overlap days, n67, q~.501 — insufficient independent support.
- H29 rapid 1m impulse fade n45, positive but sparse; H54 premarket break n1013 with near-zero net effect. These are candidate FALLBACKS, not independently validated sources of edge.
- An earlier four-parent 1-MNQ portfolio delivered 407 trades, +$2739.84 net over 2020–24 but closed DD ~$2718.12, with 58 >7-day gaps. It is NOT a qualified 50k funded strategy.

**Joint causal prediction:** an explicit day-level router may diversify opportunities by using gap continuation (H09), premarket+VWAP **consensus** (H55 AND H56, one position), and sparse fallbacks (H29, H54) when no early position exists; it may raise number of completed trades while controlling duplicate NQ exposure. Ex-ante **previous-day RTH range versus prior-20 median** is a mechanically defined context for contrasting early gap continuation and consensus. Its *incremental performance* versus a fixed baseline router H67 is falsifiable; more frequent trades alone are NOT evidence of economic edge.

## Primary economic experiment: executable MNQ proxy bracket
- Reuse exact causal parent (timestamp, side) on NQ source. **Model 1 MNQ at NQ-minute OHLC as PRICE PROXY**, $2 per point; MNQ own minute or quote-level entry/parity is **NOT** yet independently verified. Strict separate cross-instrument transfer validation required before any trading, despite common index.
- Cost NinjaTrader Free, MNQ $0.94 per side = $1.88 RT, plus **two 0.25-point adverse ticks per side** at $2/point = $2 slippage RT; **total $3.88 per position**. MFFU funded fees are different and need independent validation; no double-counting NQ parent costs.
- Previous 14 completed eligible RTH daily high-low median (computed strictly before session) defines A. Risk distance = **ceil(0.15 × A /0.25)×0.25** NQ index points, minimum one tick. Long stop entry−R, target entry+1.5R; short symmetric. Tick-round target to nearest outward tick (ceil(1.5R/0.25) ticks). Time exit = open at +30 one-minute bars after entry, latest index signal+31. Conservative intrabar rule STOP before TARGET when both touched, with stop filled at worst of stop and bar OPEN if gapped; target never receives price improvement. Contract = one MNQ; exactly ONE transaction per day, no reentry.
- Primary outcome is **net MNQ USD realized bracket P&L**, mean over all executed sessions. Secondary **same 30m time exit net MNQ P&L**, individual source attribution, activity, volatility by same-day prior features and 1.5× total cost sensitivity.
