# PDC Stop Design — Decision

Status: **NO FORMAL PROMOTION / ATR 0.30 IS A RESEARCH CANDIDATE**

Run date: 2026-10-06.

The stop grid and selection rules were preregistered before validation outcomes were inspected.

## Split

- Design: 2020-2022
- Validation: 2023-2025
- 2026 remained closed.

## Formal result

No stop candidate passed the frozen 2020-2022 design eligibility gate because the no-stop control was exceptionally strong in that period:
- expectancy: +$16.01/trade
- PF: 4.53
- max DD: $75.63

Every tested stop reduced design expectancy and/or worsened drawdown versus that control.

Therefore no stop was legitimately selected before validation, and the formal preregistered study does not promote a stop.

## Important validation finding

Despite the formal failure, ATR 0.30 showed the strongest recent-period risk-control behavior and is retained only as a **new research candidate**.

### 2023-2025

No stop:
- expectancy: -$3.64/trade
- PF: 0.84
- stress expectancy: -$5.83/trade
- max DD: $603.78
- worst trade: -$365.63

ATR 0.30:
- expectancy: **+$3.01/trade**
- PF: **1.20**
- stress expectancy: **+$0.82/trade**
- max DD: **$176.15**
- worst trade: **-$117.70**

This is approximately a 71% reduction in validation max drawdown.

Annual ATR 0.30:
- 2023: -$1.14/trade
- 2024: +$6.73/trade
- 2025: +$3.13/trade

2025 specifically:
- no stop: -$18.26/trade; total -$365.10
- ATR 0.30: +$3.13/trade; total +$62.53

Thus ATR 0.30 directly addresses the 2025 catastrophic-tail problem.

## Why it is not yet approved

ATR 0.30 did not satisfy the preregistered validation stability gate:
- only 3 of 6 validation semesters were positive, versus required 4/6.
- it was not selected on the design period before validation was viewed.

Hence promoting it now as a finished stop would be post-hoc selection.

ATR 0.35 is descriptively strongest over the full sample (PF 1.63, expectancy +$7.39, DD $233.54) but is even more clearly a full-sample hindsight observation and is not promoted.

D=2.5 is the lowest-drawdown broad compromise (DD $160.65, PF 1.38, 9/12 positive semesters), but validation PF was only about 1.14 and it is not promoted.

## Decision

- Do not approve any stop as final.
- Retain **ATR 0.30** as the most defensible new stop candidate because its value emerged in the adverse 2023-2025 environment and it materially controls tail risk.
- Do not open 2026 yet.
- Before any 2026 forward test, PDC would need to be frozen as a complete strategy including news rule, entry, target, stop, time exit, and position sizing.
