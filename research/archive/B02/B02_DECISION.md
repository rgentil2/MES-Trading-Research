# B02 Decision — Overnight & Opening Structure

All five hypotheses were frozen together before execution and evaluated with BH/FDR correction.

## H028 / R019 — Overnight Inventory Correction
- N: 255
- Mean reversion: -2.110 bps
- p-value: 0.3629
- 95% CI: [-6.656, 2.436]
- Positive years: 2/6
- Decision: REJECT

Large overnight moves did not show the pre-registered opening correction effect.

## H029 / R020 — Overnight High/Low Breakout
- N: 1,043
- Mean continuation: +0.548 bps
- p-value: 0.5498
- 95% CI: [-1.249, 2.345]
- Positive years: 4/6
- Decision: REJECT

The effect is small and statistically uncertain.

## H030 / R021 — Overnight Compression → Opening Expansion
- N: 328
- Mean opening expansion: -32.475%
- Median: -37.815%
- p-value: 3.29e-95
- BH q-value: 1.32e-94
- 95% CI: [-35.549%, -29.400%]
- Positive years: 0/6
- Decision: REJECT H030

The result is very strongly opposite to the pre-registered hypothesis. A narrow overnight was associated with an opening 30-minute range roughly 32% below its historical baseline, not with expansion.

This opposite result is recorded as a discovery only. It is not retrospectively relabeled as a passed hypothesis.

## H031 / R022 — Opening Location Reversion
- N: 599
- Mean reversion: -0.240 bps
- p-value: 0.8601
- 95% CI: [-2.912, 2.431]
- Positive years: 5/6
- Decision: REJECT

Despite 5/6 yearly signs, the aggregate effect is essentially zero.

## H032 / R023 — Efficient Overnight Trend Continuation
- Eligible events: 0
- Decision: REJECT / no events

The frozen efficiency threshold was too restrictive. It is not relaxed after seeing the result.

## Overall
No B02 primary hypothesis passes.

The strongest new information is the opposite-sign H030 discovery: overnight range compression appears to persist into the first 30 minutes of RTH.

2026 remains completely unopened.
