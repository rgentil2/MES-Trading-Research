# R001 Decision — REJECT H001/H002

## Development window

2020-01-01 through 2024-12-31 only.

The 2025 validation set and 2026 pseudo-forward set were not opened.

## Final core result

After enforcing exact elapsed-minute forward horizons:

- LOWER ±2σ event, 15m: N=5,608; mean directional return = -0.083 bps; p=0.8174; 95% CI [-0.785, 0.620].
- UPPER ±2σ event, 15m: N=6,349; mean directional return = +0.378 bps; p=0.1971; 95% CI [-0.196, 0.952].

Positive directional return represented reversion in the hypothesized direction.

## Decision

H001 and H002 do not pass the R001 development gate.

The unconditional statement "first crossing of ±2σ from session VWAP predicts 15-minute mean reversion" is rejected for this research program.

This does not claim that VWAP has no useful information in other contexts; it closes only these pre-registered hypotheses.
